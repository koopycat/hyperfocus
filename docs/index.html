Why Hyperfocus

We are early, so instead of borrowed quotes here are the numbers we actually measured on an M4 Pro.

Static screens cost nothing

Deep mode hashes each captured frame on the GPU and skips rendering when nothing changed. With the Frozen background mode the app idles at about 0.1% CPU.

Quarter-resolution capture

ScreenCaptureKit is captured at one quarter resolution — 16× fewer pixels — then upscaled by Core Animation while compositing. No CPU readback anywhere in the pipeline.

No accounts, no telemetry

Everything renders on-device. Screen captures never leave your Mac, there is no trial, and no license key to lose.
